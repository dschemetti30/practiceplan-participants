How it works
Four steps to your reservation
1

Visit the rental portal

Go to practiceplan.io or use the direct link your school or organization shared with you. You're one click from seeing what's available.

2

Create your participant account

Click Sign Up and enter your basic info. Takes about two minutes on any device — phone, tablet, or computer. No credit card required to register.

3

Find your facility

Search by zip code or the name of the school, campus, or organization. Gyms, fields, courts, auditoriums, and more — browse what's available and pick what fits.

4

Submit and get approved

Complete the required info and submit your request. Most requests are reviewed within 1-2 business days. You'll get an email the moment a decision is made.
